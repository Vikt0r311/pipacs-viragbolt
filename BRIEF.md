Virág Gábor Építő Bt. is a monolithic reinforced concrete construction company based in Szentkirályszabadja (Veszprém County, Hungary), specializing in formwork (zsaluzás), reinforcement (vasalás), and concreting (betonozás) of floor slabs (födémek), walls (falak), ring beams (koszorúk), staircases (lépcsők), and other structural elements. Founded in 2021, the company serves the Veszprém–Balaton region with a small, dedicated team. The single most important differentiator: no direct competitor in the Szentkirályszabadja–Veszprém micro-region has a modern, professional website focused specifically on monolithic reinforced concrete structural work — every competitor either has an outdated site, no site at all, or is a large generalist firm. This creates an enormous opportunity to dominate local search with a niche-focused, premium-quality web presence.

## 4. Design Direction

### Design Differentiation Answers

- **What does EVERY competitor look like?** White background, grey text, blue accents, WordPress sidebar, stock construction imagery, text-heavy pages with no hierarchy. Corporate, dated, impersonal.
- **What would make this site immediately different?** Dark background with warm amber/gold accents — the raw, textured feel of fresh concrete and steel. Industrial-premium aesthetic that says "we are specialists, not generalists."
- **What visual style has NEVER been used in this industry locally?** Dark industrial-luxury with concrete texture overlays, exposed-steel gold accents, and architectural photography composition. No Hungarian RC contractor has this.
- **What is the ONE thing a visitor remembers after 3 seconds?** A dramatic full-width hero showing a freshly poured concrete slab from an elevated angle, with the formwork still visible at the edges — overlaid with a bold, single-line Hungarian headline in a condensed industrial typeface.
- **Which non-competing industry inspires the design?** Architecture portfolio sites and premium industrial equipment brands. The feel should be closer to an architect's portfolio than a construction company brochure.

1. **https://dfraud.co/** — Dark background, bold typography, industrial feel with warm accent tones. Scroll-triggered reveals. This is the mood target.
2. **https://www.tifrfryer.com/** — Dark premium with gold accents, dramatic imagery, editorial layout. Captures the "specialist craft" feeling we want.

### Typography

- **Display font:** `Inter` weight 800 (Extra Bold) and 900 (Black) — condensed tracking (-0.02em). Used for H1, H2, hero text. Uppercase for hero headline.
- **Body font:** `Inter` weight 400 (Regular) and 500 (Medium) — standard tracking. Used for paragraph text, descriptions.
- **Size scale (mobile / desktop):**
  - H1: 32px / 56px, line-height 1.1
  - H2: 24px / 40px, line-height 1.15
  - H3: 20px / 28px, line-height 1.2
  - Body: 16px / 18px, line-height 1.6
  - Small: 14px / 14px, line-height 1.5

### Layout Philosophy

- **12-column grid** with deliberate asymmetry: content areas occupy 7 columns on one side, leaving 5 for imagery or whitespace. Never a symmetric 6-6 split.
- **Section rhythm:** Alternate between full-width dark sections and contained-width dark sections with a slightly lighter background (`#2A2A2A` vs `#1A1A1A`) to create depth.
- **No symmetric card grids.** Services displayed as staggered horizontal strips with image on alternating sides (left-right-left pattern).
- **Generous vertical spacing:** 120px between major sections on desktop, 80px on mobile.

### Hero Section

Full-viewport hero with:
- Background: A high-quality photo of a freshly poured concrete slab with visible rebar grid (placeholder until client provides real photos) — darkened with a `linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(26,26,26,0.7), rgba(26,26,26,0.95))` overlay.
- Headline: Top-left aligned (not centered), positioned at approximately 40% from top. Large, uppercase, `Inter Black`. Two lines: "MONOLIT VASBETON" / "SZERKEZETEK VESZPREMBEN" — with "VESZPREMBEN" in Rebar Amber (`#D4932A`).
- Subheadline: One line below in `Inter Regular`, Concrete Light color: "Fodémek, falak, koszorúk, lépcsők zsaluzása, vasalása és betonozása."
- CTA button: "Ajánlatot kérek" — Rebar Amber background, Concrete Black text, positioned below subheadline. On hover: background shifts to Formwork Gold (`#E8B84B`), slight Y-axis lift with box-shadow.
- Scroll indicator: Thin animated chevron-down (lucide: ChevronDown) at bottom center, subtle bounce animation.

### Patterns to Avoid

- **No white backgrounds** — every competitor uses white. This site uses exclusively dark tones.
- **No sidebar layouts** — every WordPress competitor has sidebars. Full-width only.
- **No stock hardhat photos** — every competitor uses the same stock imagery. Use real project photos or abstract concrete textures until real photos are provided.
- **No card-grid layouts** — services are never displayed as equal-sized cards in a symmetric grid.
- **No blue color anywhere** — every competitor defaults to blue. This site is warm amber/gold on dark.

### Hover States & Animations

- **Buttons:** 300ms ease background-color transition + translateY(-2px) + box-shadow appear.
- **Links:** Underline slides in from left (width animation) on hover, Rebar Amber color.
- **Cards/sections:** Scroll-triggered Framer Motion `fadeInUp` (y: 30 -> 0, opacity: 0 -> 1, duration: 0.6s, staggerChildren: 0.15s).
- **Images:** Subtle scale(1.03) on hover with overflow hidden container, 500ms ease.
- **Process steps:** Sequential reveal on scroll — each step fades in with a slight delay after the previous.
- **Counter animations:** Number count-up animation for statistics (years of experience, projects completed, etc.) triggered on scroll into viewport.

### Signature Element

**"Concrete Pour Line"** — A thin, horizontal animated line (2px height, Rebar Amber color) that extends across the full viewport width at the boundary between major sections. On scroll, the line "pours" from left to right (width animation from 0 to 100%), visually mimicking the flow of concrete. This element appears between every major section and creates a unique visual rhythm that no competitor has. Implementation: Framer Motion `useInView` trigger with `scaleX` from 0 to 1, `transformOrigin: "left"`, duration 0.8s.

## 8. Build Instructions for Claude Code

### Reference URLs to Fetch and Match

1. **https://dfraud.co/** — Dark industrial aesthetic, bold typography, scroll animations. Match this mood.
2. **https://www.tifrfryer.com/** — Premium dark with warm accents. Match the craft-specialist feeling.

Claude Code must fetch these URLs before building to understand the visual quality target.

### Patterns to Use

- Dark-on-dark section alternation (`#1A1A1A` / `#2A2A2A`) for depth
- Framer Motion `useInView` for all scroll-triggered animations
- `fadeInUp` as default entry animation (y: 30, opacity: 0 -> y: 0, opacity: 1)
- Staggered children with 150ms delay between items
- Counter animation for statistics section (count from 0 to target number)
- The "Concrete Pour Line" signature element between every major section
- Asymmetric layouts: 7/5 or 8/4 column splits, never 6/6
- Images with `overflow-hidden` container and subtle scale on hover

### Patterns to Avoid

- **No white or light backgrounds anywhere** — competitors all use white
- **No blue colors** — competitors all default to blue accents
- **No symmetric card grids** — services are staggered strips, not equal cards
- **No sidebar layouts** — full-width sections only
- **No stock hardhat imagery** — use concrete/rebar textures or real photos
- **No generic WordPress/template aesthetics** — this must feel custom-built
- **No min-h-screen on root elements**

### Special Animations / Interactions

- **Concrete Pour Line:** Amber line that scales from left to right between sections (Framer Motion `scaleX` 0 -> 1, `transformOrigin: "left"`)
- **Process timeline:** Steps reveal sequentially as user scrolls (Framer Motion stagger)
- **Gallery lightbox:** Click image -> overlay with Framer Motion `layoutId` animation
- **FAQ accordion:** Smooth height animation on expand/collapse
- **Counter:** Number count-up from 0 to target, triggered by `useInView`

### Mobile-First Considerations

- All layouts designed mobile-first, desktop enhancements via Tailwind breakpoints
- Touch-friendly tap targets (min 48px)
- Floating phone CTA fixed at bottom-right on mobile
- Navigation collapses to hamburger on mobile
- Gallery switches from masonry to single-column on mobile
- Hero text scales down cleanly (32px mobile -> 56px desktop)

### Performance Requirements

- **Lighthouse 90+** on all scores (Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO)
- All images: Next.js `<Image>` component with proper width/height/alt
- Lazy loading for below-fold images
- Font loading: `next/font` for Inter, display swap
- Minimal JS bundle — no unnecessary dependencies

### Image Optimization

- Use Next.js `<Image>` for all images with explicit dimensions
- WebP format preferred
- Placeholder: `blur` placeholder with blurDataURL for all images
- Max image width: 1920px for full-width, 960px for half-width sections
- All images must have descriptive Hungarian `alt` text with location keywords where natural

### Signature Element Implementation

The "Concrete Pour Line" is defined as a reusable React component:
- A `<motion.div>` with height 2px, background Rebar Amber (`#D4932A`)
- Uses `useInView` hook with `once: true` and `margin: "-100px"`
- Animates `scaleX` from 0 to 1, `transformOrigin: "left"`, `duration: 0.8`, `ease: "easeInOut"`
- Placed as `<ConcretePourLine />` component between every major `<section>` in each page
